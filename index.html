Allergen Advice & Dietary Information

All Bunker Brews craft syrups are produced in small artisanal batches with natural culinary botanicals, citrus extracts, and pure sugars.

Dietary Compatibility:
  • 100% Vegan & Vegetarian: No animal derivatives or bone char.
  • Gluten-Free: Formulated without cereals containing gluten.
  • Naturally Caffeine-Free: Enjoy anytime without vault sleep disruption.
  • No Artificial Preservatives or High-Fructose Corn Syrup.
Natural Botanical Ingredients:

Contains pure cane sugar, dark brown sugar, demerara sugar, pure molasses, fresh orange & lemon citrus juice and zest, ground cinnamon, nutmeg, ground coriander, star anise, and pure vanilla extract.

Free from the 14 major UK/EU allergens (Celery, Cereals containing gluten, Crustaceans, Eggs, Fish, Lupin, Milk, Molluscs, Mustard, Tree Nuts, Peanuts, Sesame, Soya, Sulphur dioxide/Sulphites).

BUNKER MIXOLOGY CODEX // CLASSIFIED RECIPES

Mixology Codex

BUNKER BASIC OR SCOUT 1 MIN

01. The Classic Vault Sparkler

The definitive way to experience pure unadulterated craft cola with crisp effervescence.

Ration Measurements:
• Bunker Syrup:30 ml
• Chilled Sparkling Water:150 ml
• Garnish:Ice + Lemon Wheel

Protocol: Pack a tumbler with fresh ice. Pour 30ml syrup over ice, top with icy sparkling water, and stir gently. Never shake!

BUNKER BASIC 2 MINS

02. Glowing Campfire Float

A nostalgic 1950s soda fountain indulgence combining velvety vanilla bean ice cream with festive spiced cola.

Ration Measurements:
• Bunker Basic:35 ml
• Sparkling Soda:120 ml
• Topping:1 Scoop Vanilla Bean Ice Cream + Nutmeg

Protocol: Mix syrup and soda in a tall chilled glass. Gently lower a large scoop of clotted vanilla ice cream on top and dust with cracked nutmeg.

BUNKER BASIC COCKTAIL

03. Wasteland Bourbon Cola

An elevated campfire cocktail where oak, charred barrel warmth, and cassia cinnamon harmonise.

Ration Measurements:
• Bunker Basic:25 ml
• Bourbon or Spiced Rum:50 ml
• Club Soda & Bitters:100 ml + 2 dashes Angostura

Protocol: Combine bourbon, syrup, and bitters in an Old Fashioned glass with a large ice sphere. Top with soda and express a flamed orange peel over the rim.

BUNKER SCOUT REFRESHING

04. The Botanical Scout Mule

A zesty, botanical fusion pairing caramelized molasses and citrus with fiery ginger beer.

Ration Measurements:
• Bunker Scout:30 ml
• Fiery Ginger Beer:120 ml
• Fresh Lime & Mint:15ml lime juice + slapped mint

Protocol: Pack a copper mug with crushed ice. Pour Scout and fresh lime. Top with fiery ginger beer (add 50ml vodka/rum for adult ration).

BUNKER SCOUT KICKSTART

05. Espresso Vault Tonic

Rich roasted espresso meets cold sparkling botanical cola for an invigorating dark crema refresher.

Ration Measurements:
• Bunker Scout:20 ml
• Fresh Double Espresso:1 Shot (30 ml)
• Sparkling Mineral Water:120 ml + Ice

Protocol: Add ice, Scout, and sparkling water into a glass. Float the hot espresso over the back of a spoon to create two striking layers.

BUNKER SCOUT CULINARY

06. Smoked Molasses Glaze

Transform your cooking with a sticky, caramelized cola glaze for meats, grilled halloumi, or roasted root veg.

Ration Measurements:
• Bunker Scout:60 ml
• Apple Cider Vinegar:2 tbsp
• Mustard & Smoked Paprika:1 tbsp Dijon + 1 tsp Paprika

Protocol: Whisk ingredients in a small pan over gentle heat for 4 minutes until thickened. Brush generously during the final 5 minutes of grilling.
